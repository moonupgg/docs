## How to Place an Order

Purchasing on MoonUP is quick and secure. Follow these simple steps to complete your order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Categories">
    Navigate to the category you're interested in, such as Accounts, Currency, Items, or Boosting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Product">
    Open the product page and carefully review the description, delivery method, warranty, and any additional information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Cart">
    Click Buy Now or Add to Cart, then proceed to checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Choose your preferred payment method and complete the transaction securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Order">
    Once your payment is confirmed, your order will begin processing. Delivery times depend on the selected product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order Status

You can monitor every stage of your purchase from your account dashboard.

**Order Statuses**

- Awaiting payment — the order is created and the item is reserved for you; complete the payment before the timer runs out.
- Awaiting delivery — payment is received and the seller is preparing your order. If the seller misses the delivery deadline, the order is cancelled automatically.
- Credentials delivered — the seller has sent your order. Check it and confirm receipt, or open a dispute if something is wrong. If you do nothing, the order completes automatically after 5 days.
- Completed — the order is closed. The warranty still applies, and you can leave a review.
- Disputed — you have opened a dispute and our team is reviewing the order.
- Refunded — the money has been returned to your MoonUP balance.
- Cancelled — the order was cancelled before delivery and the payment was released.
- Failed — the order did not go through: it was not paid in time or the item was no longer available.

If your order remains in the same status longer than expected, please contact our support team.

## Order Confirmation

After successfully completing your payment, you'll receive an order confirmation.

The confirmation includes:

- Order ID
- Purchased product
- Payment status
- Estimated delivery time
- Seller information (if applicable)

You can also find all your active and completed orders in My Orders.

If you don't receive a confirmation within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Delivery Process

Delivery methods vary depending on the type of product.

**Accounts**

Login credentials will be securely delivered through your order page.

**Currency**

The seller will deliver the currency using the selected in-game trading method.

**Items**

Items are transferred using the game's trading system.

**Boosting**

The booster will begin the service according to the agreed schedule and update the order status throughout the process.

You'll receive a notification once delivery has been completed.

## Cancel an Order

Orders may be cancelled before delivery begins.

You can request a cancellation if:

- The seller has not started processing the order.
- Delivery exceeds the expected timeframe.

Orders that have already been fully delivered or completed may not be eligible for cancellation.

To request a cancellation:

- Open My Orders.
- Select the order.
- Click Tickets.
- Click Create Ticket.
- Choose the reason for your request.
- Wait for the review by our support team.

If you need immediate assistance, open a support ticket, or reach us on Discord or by email.

<Warning>
**Cancellation window**

Orders that have already been fully delivered or completed may not be eligible for cancellation.
</Warning>
