## Order Progress

You can track the status of your boosting order at any time from My Orders. Progress updates are displayed automatically, and you'll receive notifications whenever your order status changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order Placed">
    You place your boosting order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booster Assigned">
    A professional booster is assigned to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boost In Progress">
    The booster completes your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed">
    The order is completed and ready for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule

Some boosting services can be scheduled at a time that's convenient for you.

When placing your order, you may:

- Choose your preferred start time
- Specify your availability
- Add special instructions for the booster

If no schedule is selected, the booster will begin as soon as possible after payment.

## ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival)

Each boosting service includes an estimated completion time based on the selected package.

ETA depends on:

- Current rank or level
- Selected service
- Game availability
- Queue times
- Booster schedule

Please note that ETA is an estimate and may vary due to game updates or unexpected delays.

<Tip>
**Keep in mind**

ETA is an estimate and may vary due to game updates or unexpected delays.
</Tip>

## Pause Boost

Need a break? You can request to temporarily pause your boosting order.

Common reasons include:

- You want to play on your account
- Game maintenance
- Personal schedule
- Technical issues

To request a pause, simply contact the booster through your order chat or reach out to our support team.

Boosting will resume once you're ready.

## Complete Boost

Once your boosting service has been finished, the order status will change to Completed.

Before confirming completion, we recommend:

- Checking your final rank, level, or completed objectives
- Verifying that all requested milestones have been reached
- Reviewing any notes left by the booster

If everything is correct, you can confirm completion and leave feedback.

If you experience any issues after completion, contact our support team as soon as possible.

<Note>
**Security**

We recommend changing your password and enabling 2FA after every boost for maximum account safety.
</Note>
