## Payment Methods

MoonUP supports a variety of secure payment methods to make purchasing fast and convenient.

The available payment methods are displayed during checkout and may vary depending on your region.

All transactions are processed through secure payment providers.

## Failed Payment

If your payment was unsuccessful, don't worry—most issues can be resolved quickly.

Common reasons include:

- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect payment information
- Bank security verification
- Payment provider interruption
- Transaction timeout

If your payment failed:

- Verify your payment details.
- Try another payment method.
- Contact your bank if the transaction was declined.
- Reach out to our support team if the issue persists.

You will not be charged for unsuccessful payment attempts.

## Pending Payment

A pending payment means your payment provider is still processing your transaction.

Most pending payments are confirmed within a few minutes, although some providers may take longer.

While your payment is pending:

- Your order will remain on hold.
- Delivery will begin once payment is confirmed.
- No additional action is required unless requested.

If your payment remains pending for an extended period, please contact our support team.

## Refund Policy

Customer satisfaction is our priority.

Refund eligibility depends on the order status and the type of product purchased.

You may request a refund if:

- The seller cannot complete the order.
- The purchased product is unavailable.
- The order has not been delivered.
- A technical issue prevents successful delivery.

Refund requests are reviewed individually by our support team.

Completed or successfully delivered orders may not be eligible for a refund unless covered by Buyer Protection or another applicable policy.

## Invoice

An invoice is automatically generated for every successful purchase.

Your invoice includes:

- Order ID
- Purchase date
- Product details
- Payment method
- Total amount paid
- Billing information (if provided)

You can download or view your invoice at any time from My Orders.

If you require a corrected invoice or have billing questions, please contact our support team.
